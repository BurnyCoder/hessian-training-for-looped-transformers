Hessian-trace regularization for looped transformers

A looped transformer reuses one block of layers K times. We trained two otherwise identical looped transformers to add binary numbers of 1–19 bits and tested them on 20–60 bits. One was trained normally; the other also paid λ·tr(H), the exact Hutchinson estimate of the Hessian trace (loss curvature). Same seed, same initialization, same training problems. Stage 1 compares one seed per arm and is descriptive only.
