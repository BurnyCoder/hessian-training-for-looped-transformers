The rendered PDF was inspected page by page and its reported scores and derivative values were matched to the generated evidence. The site was opened with the partial Stage 1 record and with the archived smoke replay. Its interim width selector reproduced the history, the fixed-depth view displayed the per-length intervals, and the partial page also rendered its local figure with external chart scripts disabled.
